WordPress.com includes a full set of domain management tools, allowing you to register new domains, transfer existing ones, or connect domains purchased elsewhere.

Once connected, you can manage DNS records, configure domain mapping, set up email forwarding, and take advantage of domain privacy protection—all from a user-friendly dashboard.

This page offers a starting point for working with domains on WordPress.com. Use the links below to explore detailed guides for each aspect of domain setup and management.

## Register a new domain

On WordPress.com, you can register a new domain directly through your account. When you purchase any new paid plan, you’ll also get a free domain for the first year.

[Learn more](https://wordpress.com/support/domains/register-domain/)

## Connect or transfer a domain

If you’ve already registered a domain with another provider, you can still use it with your WordPress.com site. You can either connect the domain or transfer it to WordPress.com to manage everything in one place.

[Learn more](https://wordpress.com/support/domains/connect-existing-domain/)

## Manage your DNS records

DNS records control how your domain behaves—like directing visitors to your site or connecting your domain to email services. This guide explains how to add, remove, and update DNS records for your domain.

[Learn more](https://wordpress.com/support/domains/connect-existing-domain/)

## Domain pricing and available TLDs

You can find the current pricing for one year of standard domain registration in the guide linked below. With any annual or multi-year WordPress.com plan, your first year of domain registration is free.

[Learn more](https://wordpress.com/support/domains/domain-pricing-and-available-tlds/)

## Change a domain

Domain names can’t be edited once registered. Instead, you can add a new domain to your site and set it as the primary one. This guide will help you decide the best next step based on your needs.

[Learn more](https://wordpress.com/support/domains/change-a-domain/)

## Add email to your domain

Create a professional email address using your domain name—great for building trust and strengthening your brand. This guide walks you through how to set it up.

[Learn more](https://wordpress.com/support/add-email/)

## Email forwarding

Email forwarding lets you use your domain in an email address and have messages sent to your existing inbox. This guide shows how to enable this free feature.

[Learn more](https://wordpress.com/support/add-email/email-forwarding/)

## Secure your domain with SSL

An SSL certificate helps keep your site and your visitors’ data secure. Every domain on WordPress.com includes one at no extra cost. This guide shows you how to view and verify it.

[Learn more](https://wordpress.com/support/domains/https-ssl/)
