On sites hosted with WordPress.com, Jetpack Scan automatically detects security threats and vulnerabilities in plugins, themes, and uploaded files. It relies on threat data from WPScan and the WordPress.com security team to help protect your site from malware and potential breaches. Unlike on other hosting platforms where Jetpack Scan requires a separate purchase, it’s included with your WordPress.com plan at no extra cost.

Use the guides below to learn more about how Jetpack Scan works and how you can take additional steps to keep your site secure.

## About Jetpack Scan

Learn how Jetpack Scan works to protect your site and give you peace of mind.

[Learn more](https://jetpack.com/support/scan/)

## Keep your site safe and secure

Keeping your website and personal data secure is a top priority. This guide explains how WordPress.com helps protect your site and information, and outlines additional steps you can take to strengthen your site’s security.

[Learn more](https://wordpress.com/support/security/)

## Jetpack on plugin-enabled sites

The Jetpack plugin comes pre-installed on all WordPress.com sites and includes essential tools to help secure, speed up, and grow your site. This guide outlines the features available with Business and Commerce plans.

[Learn more](https://wordpress.com/support/jetpack-on-plugin-enabled-sites/)
