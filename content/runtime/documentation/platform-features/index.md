Unlock the full potential of your site with WordPress.com’s powerful platform features. From domain management to real-time backups, these tools are built to improve your site’s functionality, security, and performance—giving you a smooth and reliable web experience.

## Domain management

WordPress.com provides comprehensive domain management features to help users easily manage their website domains. With WordPress.com, users can register a new domain, transfer an existing domain, or connect a domain they already own.

[Learn more](/docs/platform-features/domain-management/)

## Real time backup

VaultPress Backup by Jetpack automatically saves every change and helps you get back online quickly with one‑click restores. This guide will show you how to access your site’s backups and restore your website to any previous point in time.

[Learn more](/docs/platform-features/real-time-backup-restore/)

## Storage

When you upload images and other files to your website, they are stored in your site’s *Media*. This guide will show you how to check your site’s available plan storage, increase your storage capacity, and free up space.

[Learn more](/docs/platform-features/storage/)

## Sitemaps

Boost SEO and site navigation with an automatically-generated sitemap, and understand your role in refining visibility for search engines and users alike.

[Learn more](/docs/platform-features/sitemaps/)

## Account security

We secure your site and data through strong encryption, firewalls, monitoring, and regular security testing, and all WordPress.com customers have access to extra security settings, like two-step and security key authentication. Together, we can keep your sites safe and secure.

[Learn more](https://developer.wordpress.com/docs/platform-features/account-security/)

## Brute force attack protection

Brute Force Attack Protection on WordPress.com blocks unwanted login attempts from traditional and distributed brute force login attacks. This guide explains how to use Brute Force Attack Protection on your WordPress.com website.

[Learn more](https://wordpress.com/support/brute-force-attack-protection/)

## Jetpack Scan

On WordPress.com sites, Jetpack Scan automatically checks for security threats in plugins, themes, and uploaded files. It uses data from WPScan and the WordPress.com security team, and unlike other hosts, it’s included with your plan at no extra cost.

[Learn more](https://developer.wordpress.com/docs/platform-features/jetpack-scan/)

## Jetpack Boost

Jetpack Boost supercharges your WordPress website’s performance and improves web vital scores for better SEO. This guide explains how to use Jetpack Boost on your WordPress.com website.

[Learn more](https://wordpress.com/support/check-your-sites-performance/)
