As of May 23, 2025, we are not accepting new WordPress.com Marketplace partnership submissions at this time.

If you’re a vendor interested in selling your products through WordPress.com, please register through the [WooCommerce Vendor Signup](https://woocommerce.com/vendor-signup). This has the added benefit that your product might qualify for getting listed on [WooCommerce.com](https://woocommerce.com/). Make sure you note that you want to list your product in WordPress.com.

If you require licensing validation, you’ll need to integrate with [our Licensing API](https://developer.wordpress.com/wordpress-com-marketplace/vendor-apis/).

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I provide plugin updates?**

We’ll provide access to a vendor dashboard where you can upload the updated plugin as a zip file. Our platform will then propagate the update to end users’ sites.

**How is the plugin installed on the end user’s site?**

When a WordPress.com customer purchases your product in our Marketplace, we’ll load the zip file you provided and install it on their site.

**What will be the process for handling support queries?**

Please see:

- [Support Best Practices](https://developer.wordpress.com/wordpress-com-marketplace/support-best-practices/)
- [Supporting WordPress.com Plugins](https://developer.wordpress.com/wordpress-com-marketplace/supporting-wordpress-com-plugins/)
